\section{Prerequisites}

Arfken\cite{arfken2012} is excellent background for these notes.
Look there for undefined terms like \textit{vector space} or
\textit{differentiable manifold}. We adhere to the following terse
definitions in the rest of these notes.

\begin{definition}[Lie Algebra]
    A Lie algebra is a vector space $\mathfrak{g}$ equipped with a
    bilinear, anticommutative, binary operation
    $[,]:\mathfrak{g}\times\mathfrak{g}\rightarrow\mathfrak{g}$ that satisfies the
    Jacobi identity:
    \begin{equation*}
        [X,[Y,Z]]+[Z,[X,Y]+[Y,[Z,X]]=0
    \end{equation*}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Lie Group]
    A Lie group is a group and also a differentiable manifold.
\end{definition}

\noindent It bears emphasis that a Lie algebra is not a group under the Lie bracket and
is not a differentiable manifold.
